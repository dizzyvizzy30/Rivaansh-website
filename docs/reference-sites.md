# Reference-site review

Reviewed 2026-10-03 as field and interface references for Rivaansh ENT. These sites are not content sources. Their
service lists, clinical claims, patient stories, articles, photographs and wording must not be copied or treated as
evidence that Rivaansh offers the same care.

## What each reference contributes

| Website | Useful pattern | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|
| [Bharti ENT Care](https://bhartientcare.com/) | Prominent appointment policy; clear distinction between diagnosis, treatment and post-operative care; practical facility information | Mega-menu depth, homepage service inventory, counters, testimonials, superlatives and repeated promotional sections |
| [Aashwi ENT](https://aashwient.com/) | Hours and booking are visible early; access, rooms, parking and facilities answer practical visit questions | Long hospital introduction, generic claims, large surgery list and a form unless delivery and consent are implemented |
| [Royal Pearl / Skull Base Surgery](https://www.skullbasesurgery.net/) | Strong specialist positioning and direct appointment contact | Specialist procedures, training content, result claims and multiple unexplained phone numbers |
| [Indorewala ENT](https://www.indorewala.com/) | Clear ear/nose/throat grouping; location-specific facility and access detail; visible appointment path | Very long homepage, repeated sections, exhaustive procedures, patient stories and “best/world-class” language |
| [Jain ENT Hospital](https://jainenthospital.org/) | Specialty proof and dedicated hospital/facility information | Rankings, international-patient sales language, testimonials and equipment lists without clinic confirmation |
| [Shruti ENT Hospital](https://www.shrutienthospital.co.in/) | Compact team and contact presentation; a focused specialty story | Unverified surgery counts, broad service claims and content that belongs to a larger hospital rather than this clinic |
| [Eyeconic Eye Clinic](https://eyeconiceyeclinic.com/) | Scannable service categories and unusually useful directions, nearby landmarks, transport and parking information | Review feeds, satisfaction claims, large counters, outcome-led language and search-keyword repetition |

## Resolution for Rivaansh

The existing Rivaansh strategy remains stronger for a single-clinician neighbourhood clinic:

1. Home starts with the words patients use for their problem, then explains the visit, shows factual clinician proof,
   and closes with location and booking.
2. Treatments separates conditions from procedures. Procedures do not lead the homepage.
3. Doctor & Clinic owns the full doctor profile, clinic photos, equipment and hygiene facts, so Home does not repeat
   a full biography or gallery.
4. Visit Us owns timings, access, fees, first-visit steps and FAQ. Missing clinic facts remain unpublished until the
   owner confirms them.
5. Booking uses direct phone actions until a form or WhatsApp channel has a real responder and delivery workflow.

## Reference-led additions made

- Added a visible “problem not listed?” phone path after the symptom finder and after the procedure list. This avoids
  pretending the on-page list is exhaustive.
- Strengthened the homepage closing section with what to have ready and direct Call, Directions and Booking-details
  actions at the point where a visitor is deciding to attend.
- Kept access, parking, lift, fees, payment, insurance, equipment and appointment-policy fields in the content model,
  but they remain hidden on the live site until confirmed.

## Explicitly rejected

- Testimonials, ratings, patient counts, success rates and before/after material.
- “Best”, “leading”, “world-class”, “painless”, “permanent” or guaranteed-outcome language.
- Large homepage procedure catalogues, repeated doctor biographies and duplicate contact sections.
- Any service, test, equipment, branch, insurance arrangement or 24-hour availability not confirmed in writing.
- Contact forms that do not yet have secure delivery, consent text and an accountable responder.
