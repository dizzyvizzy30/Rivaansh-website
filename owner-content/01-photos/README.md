# Photos — one photo session (about 45–60 minutes)

**Website pages:** Home, Doctor & Clinic, Visit Us, Treatments, condition pages

**Developer note:** each folder name is a photo spot on the website. The chosen photo is saved as
`src/assets/images/slots/<folder-name>.jpg` and appears automatically (see `src/data/photo-slots.ts`).

Walk through the clinic in this order and put each photo in the folder with the same name. Each folder has a
`what-to-shoot.md` with the details. On a preview link, the page **/pages/photos-needed.html** shows the same list with
the exact spot on the website where each photo goes.

## Tips
- Daylight, lights on, phone at eye level. Wipe the lens first.
- Take three of each — we pick the best.
- Send the original files (not WhatsApp copies, which lose quality). Google Drive or a USB cable works.

## Consent
- **No patients** in any photo, and **no patient names or reports on screens**.
- Staff who appear must agree to their photo being on the website.

## What to do
1. Take the photos and put them in the folders above.
2. Answer the three questions in `text.md`.
