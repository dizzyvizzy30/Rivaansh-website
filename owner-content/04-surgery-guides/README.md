# Before & after surgery guides

**Website pages:** Before & After Surgery (/pages/care.html) and the procedure sections on condition pages

**Time needed:** about 15 minutes

**Developer note:** updates go into `src/data/care.ts (per-procedure sections are added in Phase 2)`.

Send photos or scans of the instruction sheets you already give to patients before and after each operation.

## What to do
1. Answer the questions in `text.md` (type under each "Your answer").
2. Put files in the folders below — the folder name says what goes in it.

- `handouts/` — One photo/scan per handout, named after the operation (e.g. `tonsillectomy-aftercare.jpg`).

**Photo tips:** take photos in daylight with the phone held steady; send the original files (not WhatsApp-compressed
copies) if possible; no watermarks. Name each file after what it shows, e.g. `reception-waiting-area.jpg`.
**Consent:** no identifiable patients. Staff who appear in photos must agree to their photo being on the website.
