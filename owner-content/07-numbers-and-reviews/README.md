# Numbers and patient reviews

**Website pages:** Home (numbers under the top banner, "What patients say") and Doctor & Clinic (#numbers, #reviews)

**Time needed:** about 30 minutes, plus collecting consent forms over a few weeks

**Developer note:** updates go into `src/data/proof.ts`.

Numbers and patient words build trust — but Indian medical-ethics rules (NMC) restrict how doctors advertise. To keep
the doctor safe, the website only shows:

- **Numbers you can back up** — each one with how it was counted (for example "operation register, 2015–2026").
  Success rates and satisfaction percentages are only shown if they come from a real, documented survey.
- **Patients' own words, with their written consent** — never edited except to shorten, never invented, no promises
  of results. The easiest source is the clinic's Google reviews (we link to them).
- Please ask the doctor to confirm they are comfortable with patient quotes on the website before sending any.

## What to do
1. Answer the questions in `text.md`.
2. For each patient quote, get the patient to sign `consent-form.md` (print it, or send it on WhatsApp and keep the
   signed reply), and keep the signed copy at the clinic. Don't send us the patient's phone number or address.
