# Numbers and patient reviews — hold

No action is needed for this folder. Patient counts, surgery counts, testimonials and rating badges are not displayed
on the website under the current conservative reading of the NMC advertising rules. Written consent protects patient
privacy, but it does not by itself resolve the separate restriction on promotional publicity.

The old questions and consent template are retained here only as an archive. Do not collect or publish this material
for the website without case-specific advice from the relevant medical council or an Indian healthcare lawyer.
