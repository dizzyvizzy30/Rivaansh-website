# Owner content — what we need from the clinic

The website has been reorganised into four sections — **Treatments · Doctor & Clinic · Visit Us · Health Tips** — plus
Book and Before & After Surgery (see `docs/site-restructure-plan.md`). This folder collects the facts and photos for
it. **We write the medical pages; the doctor only reviews them.** Total owner time is about 5 hours.

## Folders

| Folder | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| `00-clinic-facts` | Clinic facts (used on every page) | about 30 minutes |
| `01-photos` | Photos (one phone photo session) | about 1 hour |
| `02-doctor-profile` | Doctor profile | about 20 minutes |
| `03-conditions-review` | Condition pages — review our drafts | about 15 minutes per page |
| `04-surgery-guides` | Before & after surgery guides | about 15 minutes |
| `05-health-tips-review` | Health tips — review our drafts | about 1 hour in total |
| `06-faq` | Frequently asked questions | about 20 minutes (the receptionist can answer this) |

## How it works
1. Open a folder, read its `README.md`, and answer the questions in `text.md`.
2. Put photos in the named sub-folders (the name says where each photo is used).
3. Send the folder back (zip it, or share it on Google Drive). Keep your own copy of the original photos — they are not
   stored on GitHub.
4. For draft pages (folders 03 and 05) you will get preview links to read on your phone. Reply "OK" or with corrections
   on WhatsApp.

2. One public phone number (and whether it is also the WhatsApp number).

11. Agree to review two condition pages a week.
12. Staff consent for photos showing faces.

## What changed on the website (and why)
Removed until confirmed, to follow Indian medical-advertising rules: patient testimonials with made-up names, the
"15+ years / 10,000+ patients / 98% satisfaction" numbers, the template doctors (Dr. Emily Carter, Dr. Ben Hanson),
the stock "doctor" photo, unconfirmed services (cochlear implants, speech therapy, sleep apnoea clinic, smell & taste),
the second "About" page that named a different doctor, the Unjha sample address, the online-consultation page, and the
forms (they did not send anywhere — they return once WhatsApp or a Google Sheet is set up).
