Ear wax is not dirt. It protects the ear canal, traps dust and slowly moves out of the ear on its own. Most people never need to clean inside their ears.

## Why cotton buds cause problems

- They push wax deeper, where it can harden and block the ear.
- They can scratch the ear canal and start an infection.
- Pushed too far, they can tear the eardrum.

Clean only the outer ear with a towel after bathing. The same applies to hairpins, keys, matchsticks and pen caps.

## Roadside ear cleaning

Ear cleaners in markets use tools that are not sterile and work without proper light or training. This can cause infections, scratches and holes in the eardrum. Please avoid it.

## Is it safe to put oil in the ear?

Do **not** put oil or drops in the ear if:

- the ear is painful or discharging,
- you have, or may have, a hole in the eardrum, or
- you have had ear surgery or have grommets.

If wax is blocking your hearing, ask the doctor before using softening drops.

## When to see a doctor

See a doctor if your ear feels blocked, hearing drops, the ear hurts or discharges, or you hear ringing. The doctor can examine the ear and remove wax safely when it needs removing.
