Many ear, nose and throat problems — a blocked nose, a mild earache, a sore throat with a cold — get better on their own within a week or two. Some symptoms, though, should be checked sooner.

## Go to the nearest emergency department now if there is

- **Difficulty breathing**, noisy breathing, or being unable to swallow saliva
- **A nosebleed that won't stop** after 15 minutes of firmly pinching the soft part of the nose while leaning forward
- **A button battery, magnet or sharp object** in the ear or nose — button batteries can cause burns within hours
- **Sudden dizziness with weakness, numbness, slurred speech or double vision**
- **Swelling around the eye or behind the ear with fever**

## See an ENT doctor within a day or two for

- **Sudden hearing loss in one ear** — treatment works best when started early
- Severe ear pain with discharge or fever
- A sore throat that makes it very hard to swallow, especially if it is worse on one side

## Book an appointment if you have

- A **neck lump lasting more than three weeks**
- **Hoarseness lasting more than three weeks**, especially if you smoke or chew tobacco
- A blocked nose on **one side only**, or with bloody discharge
- Hearing that is gradually getting worse, or ringing in one ear
- Repeated sore throats, ear infections or sinus problems
- Snoring with pauses in breathing, or daytime sleepiness

If you are not sure, call the clinic and describe the problem.
