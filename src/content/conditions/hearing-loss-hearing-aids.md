## What it is

There are two main types of hearing loss, and some people have both:

- **Conductive hearing loss** — sound is blocked before it reaches the inner ear, for example by wax, fluid behind the eardrum, an ear infection, a hole in the eardrum, or stiffening of the small hearing bones.
- **Sensorineural hearing loss** — the inner ear or hearing nerve is affected, most often by age or loud noise, and sometimes by infections, certain medicines or family history.

## Common symptoms

- Asking people to repeat themselves, especially in noisy places or on the phone
- Turning the TV up louder than others like
- Ringing or buzzing in the ears (tinnitus)
- In children: not responding to their name, delayed speech, or problems at school

## When to see a doctor

See an ENT doctor if you or your family notice hearing getting worse, if one ear is worse than the other, or if hearing loss comes with ear discharge, pain or dizziness.

### When it's urgent

**Sudden hearing loss in one ear** — over a few hours or days, with no obvious cause such as a cold — should be seen within 24 hours. Treatment works best when it is started early.

## What happens at your visit

- Examination of the ear canal and eardrum
- Simple tuning-fork tests
- A hearing test (audiometry), and a test of how the eardrum moves (tympanometry)

## Treatment options

- **Removing wax** or treating an infection or fluid behind the eardrum.
- **Surgery** for some kinds of conductive hearing loss, such as repairing a hole in the eardrum.
- **Hearing aids** for hearing loss that cannot be fixed with medicines or surgery. A hearing test is used to choose and adjust the hearing aid, followed by a trial period and follow-up visits.
- For very severe hearing loss, the doctor can advise on whether a **cochlear implant** assessment would help.

## Questions patients ask

### Will a hearing aid make my hearing normal again?
Hearing aids make sounds louder and clearer, but hearing will not be exactly as it was. Most people need a few weeks of regular use to get used to them.

### Can age-related hearing loss be prevented?
It cannot be fully prevented, but protecting your ears from loud noise and treating hearing loss early helps you stay part of conversations.

### Should I wait until my hearing is very bad before getting help?
No. Getting a hearing test early makes it easier to adjust to treatment.
