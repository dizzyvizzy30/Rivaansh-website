## What it is

- **Allergic rhinitis** - sneezing, an itchy, runny or blocked nose and watery eyes, often triggered by dust, pollen, pets or weather changes.
- **Sinusitis** - inflammation of the air spaces around the nose. Short-term sinusitis often follows a cold; long-term (chronic) sinusitis lasts more than 12 weeks.
- **A bent septum** (the wall between the nostrils) or **swollen turbinates** (the bony ridges inside the nose) that narrow the airway.
- **Nasal polyps** - soft swellings that grow from the lining of the nose and sinuses.
- **Overuse of decongestant nasal sprays**, which can cause a rebound blocked nose.

## Common symptoms

- Blocked nose, often worse on one side
- Runny nose or mucus running down the back of the throat
- Facial pressure or pain, and headache
- Reduced sense of smell
- Sneezing and itching (with allergy)

## When to see a doctor

See an ENT doctor if symptoms last more than a few weeks, keep coming back, are on one side only, or affect your sleep or sense of smell.

### When it's urgent

Seek urgent care for **swelling or redness around the eye, double vision or changes in vision, a severe headache with fever or a stiff neck, or confusion**.

People with uncontrolled diabetes, or who have recently taken steroids, should see a doctor **the same day** if they notice blackish nasal discharge or crusts, facial pain or numbness, or facial swelling.

## What happens at your visit

- Examination of the nose, including a thin camera (nasal endoscopy) to see deeper inside
- A CT scan of the sinuses when surgery is being considered or complications are suspected
- Allergy assessment when allergy is likely

## Treatment options

- **Saline (salt-water) nasal rinses** to clear mucus.
- **Steroid nasal sprays**, used regularly and with the right technique.
- **Antihistamines** and avoiding known triggers for allergy.
- **Decongestant sprays** only for a few days at a time.
- **Antibiotics** only when a bacterial infection is likely.

### Septoplasty

An operation through the nostrils to straighten a bent septum and improve breathing. It may be combined with reducing swollen turbinates.

### Endoscopic sinus surgery

An operation done through the nose with a thin camera, with no cut on the face. It opens blocked sinus drainage pathways and removes polyps when medicines have not helped.

## Questions patients ask

### Are steroid nasal sprays safe for long-term use?
At the doses prescribed, nasal steroid sprays are widely used for long periods. Use them as your doctor advises.

### Will my allergy go away?
Allergies can be controlled well, though they may not go away completely. Treatment aims to keep symptoms under control.

### Can polyps come back after surgery?
They can. Continuing nasal sprays after surgery helps reduce this.
